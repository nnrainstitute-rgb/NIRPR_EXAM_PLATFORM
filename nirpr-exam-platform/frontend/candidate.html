NIRPR, based at the University of Ibadan in Ibadan, Nigeria, is the Technical Support Organization of the Nigerian Nuclear Regulatory Authority (NNRA). It supports radiation protection and nuclear safety through training, research, technical services, and the maintenance of national radiation standards.
